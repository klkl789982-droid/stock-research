## Inputs and timing
The existing free official daily archive provided 12,700 distinct earlier bars from the Sep 22 seed before each latest series began. Both seed hashes and the protocol hash are recorded. Added dates start Aug 29 2025. The 82 pre-2026 sessions are supported by observations of at least 300 names; they are a research session index, not a certified historical exchange calendar. Gross targets only for this extension; no simulated 2025 tax. The same 2026 sessions, v1 eligibility and existing cash account are used in evaluation. No conflicting overlap at splice. The latest constituent and eligible set is retrospective; delisted and no-longer-eligible names cannot be recovered from price history. Separate the matched published-timing sample from reconstructed outcomes; the new candidates were never published on those past dates.

All nine inputs use the same validated 21-session OHLCV mask and are available only after the assumed next-business-day daily release, not at the signal close. Training has 133,799 complete stock-dates out of 157,605 possible including warmup, ineligible names and history gaps. Features: transient return (5), 20-session return relative to equally weighted eligible peers on the same market (not an official index), positive-day persistence, realized volatility, drawdown from trailing high, mean close × volume (liquidity proxy, not exchange turnover), trailing volume ratio, a new SMA5/20 crossover and same-market trailing return capped at ±20% then scaled. Redundant raw momentum/price-position series, uncertified industry labels and undated company grades are not extra inputs. Prior values never use future prices apart from the documented vintage/constituent limitation. Dividends, small corporate actions and actual queue fills are not proven.

## Fitting and interpretation
R3 estimates signed gross returns (losses included). P3 estimates close above entry open, not positive after costs. Zero is non-up. Both have a free intercept, L2 0.02; all continuous stock features are same-day percentiles in [-1,1]. Probability model trains before a purged 20-mature-signal-day calibration tail, then fits one intercept shift; no test-day rate, scaling fit or label in that shift. The rising fraction of the test pool is the descriptive denominator, not a tradable oracle forecast. For missing TOP labels compute the difference to the pool only on the very same complete TOP dates. IC and Brier use all pairwise-valid observations each day. Tie-aware daily AUC and mean squared error decompose market-rate shift from within-day discrimination; the Brier decomposition is the mean of squared daily biases plus the residual centered squared error. Calibration cannot improve AUC for a fixed day.

V1 R1 is a shrunken historical conditional-return curve of the recent 5-day return percentile, **not** an unconditional prediction that the last return will repeat. P2 averages conditional price-up rates, not recent winning trade sizes. Reproduced v1 raw Brier and labels on real archived prices. Original v1 shows means for available TOP baskets and all available pool days separately: their difference is not necessarily paired if a TOP basket is missing. V2 uses paired daily differences; v1 reports remain unchanged.
